## 🚀 EmPay – Smart HRMS

EmPay is a modern, all-in-one Human Resource Management System (HRMS) built to simplify and automate core HR operations in a single platform.

It streamlines key processes such as employee management, attendance tracking, leave workflows, and payroll processing—ensuring accuracy, efficiency, and transparency across the organization.

Designed with real-world business logic, EmPay handles salary calculations, statutory deductions (PF, PT), and attendance-based payroll seamlessly. Its role-based access system enables Admins, HR, Payroll Officers, and Employees to interact with the platform securely and efficiently.

Ideal for startups, SMEs, and institutions, EmPay offers a scalable and user-friendly solution to manage workforce operations, reduce manual effort, and support better decision-making.

### ✨ Key Features

* Role-based access control (Admin, HR, Payroll, Employee)
* Attendance & leave management
* Automated payroll with PF & PT calculations
* Payslip generation & reporting
* Dashboard with analytics and system insights
